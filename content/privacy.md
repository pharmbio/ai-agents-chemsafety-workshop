*Please check this text with your organisation's data protection officer before registration opens.*

## What we collect

When you register we collect your name, email address, affiliation, sector, career stage, whether you attend on site or online, dietary requirements (on-site only), the topics you are interested in, and any question you send to the panel.

## Why

We use this information only to organise the workshop: to plan lunch and coffee, send the Zoom link to online participants, send practical information, and prepare the discussion. Questions sent to the panel may be read out without your name.

## Who can see it

Only the workshop organisers at Uppsala University. Aggregated numbers (for example how many people attended) may be reported to the funders. Your data is not sold or shared for other purposes.

## How long we keep it

Registration data is deleted no later than six months after the workshop.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting the organisers. <!-- Add a link to Uppsala University's page on personal data here. -->Uppsala University's general information on how it processes personal data is available on [uu.se](https://www.uu.se/en).

## Stored in your browser

This site stores your starred sessions and whether you have voted in the poll in your own browser (local storage). Nothing of this is sent to us. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.
